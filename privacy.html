Privacy Policy

English

1. Who publishes the game?

Lulipo: Match & Pop is published by Yavuz Balcı under the Lulipo Games name. For privacy questions, contact yavuz@presult.com.tr.

2. Information used by the Android game

This release does not collect or transmit player personal data to us or to an advertising or analytics provider. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising identifier, or payment details. The Android package has no Internet permission, and its game content and audio are included in the installation.

The game stores your language and difficulty preferences locally on your device so that those choices can be remembered. Gameplay is processed on the device. A new game session starts at level 1; there is no cloud-saved player profile.

3. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or share player data from this Android test release. No live ad, payment, or analytics software development kit is included.

Google Play and your device platform operate separately from the game. Their handling of store account information, downloads, device diagnostics, or other platform services is governed by their own policies, including Google's Privacy Policy. This policy does not describe all data processed by Google or your device manufacturer.

4. If you contact support

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond to and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary sensitive information. You may contact the same address to request deletion of your support correspondence; information may need to be retained where required by applicable law.

5. Local storage and deletion

Local preferences remain on the device until you change them or remove the app's data. You can reset them through Android Settings → Apps → Lulipo → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the game. There is no player account or server-held player profile to delete. Clearing game data does not delete an email you separately sent to support; use the contact address above for that request.

6. Intended audience

The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. It is not designed specifically for children under 13, and this release does not ask users to submit personal information.

7. Changes

This policy describes the current Android closed-test release. Before introducing live advertising, real purchases, analytics, accounts, or other new data flows, we will update the app's privacy information and applicable store disclosures. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
