Lulipo: Match & Pop
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · iOS
Package: com.lulipogames.lulipo

About this release: The match-3 game works offline. The iOS app offers optional rewarded video ads that require an internet connection. It does not offer in-app purchases or require a player account. Coins earned in the game have no monetary value.

English

1. Who publishes the game?

Lulipo: Match & Pop is published by Yavuz Balcı under the Lulipo Games name. For privacy questions, contact yavuz@presult.com.tr.

2. Information used by the iOS game

Gameplay and game progress are processed on your device. The app does not request access to precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or payment details. When you use the advertising feature or the app checks which privacy choices apply, Google may process technical and advertising data as described below.

The game stores your language and difficulty preferences locally on your device so that those choices can be remembered. Gameplay is processed on the device. A new game session starts at level 1; there is no cloud-saved player profile.

3. Sharing and third-party services

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads and Google User Messaging Platform manages the applicable privacy choices. Google may receive your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device or app identifiers, crash and performance diagnostics, ad views and ad interactions. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent invalid activity, and diagnose and improve its services. Lulipo disables ad personalization and does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If the home screen shows Ad privacy choices, you can use that option to revisit your choices. You can report inappropriate ads from the home screen.

Apple App Store and your device platform operate separately from the game. Their handling of store account information, downloads, device diagnostics, or other platform services is governed by their own policies, including Apple Privacy Policy. This policy does not describe all data processed by Apple or your device manufacturer.

4. If you contact support

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond to and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary sensitive information. You may contact the same address to request deletion of your support correspondence; information may need to be retained where required by applicable law.

5. Local storage and deletion

Local preferences remain on the device until you change them or remove the app's data. To reset these preferences, delete the game using iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Lulipo → Delete App. There is no player account or server-held player profile to delete. Clearing game data does not delete an email you separately sent to support; use the contact address above for that request.

6. Intended audience

The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. It is not designed specifically for children under 13, and this release does not ask users to submit personal information.

7. Changes

This policy describes the current iOS release. Before introducing materially different data flows, we will update the app's privacy information and applicable store disclosures. The effective date above identifies the current policy.

5. Cihazdaki veriler ve silme

Yerel tercihler, siz değiştirene veya uygulama verilerini kaldırana kadar cihazınızda kalır. iPhone Ayarlar → Genel → iPhone Saklama Alanı → Lulipo → Uygulamayı Sil yoluyla sıfırlayabilirsiniz. Silinecek bir oyuncu hesabı veya sunucuda tutulan oyuncu profili bulunmaz. Oyun verilerini silmek, ayrıca desteğe gönderdiğiniz e-postayı silmez; bunun için yukarıdaki iletişim adresini kullanın.
